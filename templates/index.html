About the magazine

JIS Magazine is a student-led publication from Jubail International School. It is written entirely by students, for students. We cover school events, student perspectives and community moments.

Our aim is to inform, inspire and connect our school community through student journalism.

In each issue

Student events
Articles on the latest happenings around campus, from events to achievements.
Awareness days
Pieces marking awareness days and the causes behind them.
Meet the team
Get to know the students who write and edit the magazine.
Photos
Pictures from school events and everyday student life.
